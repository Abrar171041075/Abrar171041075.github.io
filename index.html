Biography:

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate and currently serve as a Junior Instructor in the Computer Science program at Asian University for Women, Bangladesh. My research lies at the intersection of deep learning, medical image analysis, multimodal learning, computer vision, and trustworthy AI.

My work focuses on developing learning systems that extract and integrate clinically meaningful information from complex biomedical data, including MRI, X-ray, histopathological, and retinal fundus images. I am particularly interested in pathology-preserving feature extraction, explainable AI (XAI), knowledge distillation, and federated learning for healthcare applications.

I am actively seeking PhD opportunities in medical image processing, multimodal representation learning, and interpretable AI systems for healthcare.

Ongoing Work

Master's Thesis: Pathology Preserving Lesion-Vessel Interaction Learning for Progression-Aware Diabetic Retinopathy Grading

Investigating retinal biomarker characteristics and lesion-vessel structural relationships to capture clinically relevant pathological and vascular information across different stages of diabetic retinopathy. Integrating vascular abnormalities with progression-aware ordinal learning to model ordered disease severity transitions, while exploring knowledge distillation to transfer diagnostic representations to lightweight deployment models.
